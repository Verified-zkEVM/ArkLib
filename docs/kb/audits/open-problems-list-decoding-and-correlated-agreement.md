# Paper Audit: Open Problems in List Decoding and Correlated Agreement

This page records a paper-to-ArkLib audit for *Open Problems in List Decoding and Correlated
Agreement*.

**Statement numbers on this page follow the 2026-07-06 source** (the authors' current build).
They were previously keyed to the 2026-04-08 PDF, in which the §4 MCA information-set lower bound
was an unnumbered proposition; it is `Lemma 4.16` in the current build, so every §4 item from
4.17 onward shifted by one. §2, §3, §5, §6, and the appendices are unaffected. When re-checking a
row, use the current source — see [`../papers/ABF26.md`](../papers/ABF26.md) § *Version Notes*.

The goal is to list the paper's named formal items and check whether each one is already present in
ArkLib, missing, or present in a materially different form.

## Status Legend

- `present`: there is a close match in ArkLib.
- `present-but-different`: the underlying concept exists, but the interface, statement shape, or
  abstraction level differs materially from the paper.
- `present-but-incomplete`: the relevant theorem/symbol exists, but is still `sorryAx`-tainted.
  The test is membership of the declaration in `scripts/axiom_baseline.json`'s `sorry` list, not
  the presence of an unrelated `sorry` somewhere in the cited file.
- `partial`: a strict part or specialization is present, but the full paper item is missing.
- `missing`: no close formalization was found. Where a supporting layer exists but does not by
  itself constitute the paper item, the `Lean refs` column says so.
- `out-of-scope`: the paper item is not a formalization target at all — either the paper declines
  to state it, or it is downstream policy/profile material rather than reusable mathematics.

## Notes

- Rows follow the theorem-like items extracted from the PDF, plus named facts and remarks when they
  materially affect the comparison.
- Lean references are given as symbol names plus direct file links, and name declarations that
  exist in the tree. Planned names are not recorded here.
- In several places ArkLib has a more general or more reusable abstraction than the paper.
  Those are marked `present-but-different` rather than `missing`.
- Open items are listed as `missing` or `partial`. For the narrative account of what remains, see
  [`../papers/ABF26.md`](../papers/ABF26.md) § *Open Formalization Gaps*.
- **Verify a `present-but-incomplete` row before trusting it.** These rows are the ones that go
  stale fastest: a proof lands and the row is not refreshed. Seven statements are admitted as of
  this revision — Theorems 3.6, 3.11, 3.12, 4.12, 4.13, 4.17, and 5.4. Everything else marked
  `present` is axiom-clean. Cross-check with:
  `python3 -c "import json;print([d for d in json.load(open('scripts/axiom_baseline.json'))['sorry'] if d.endswith('.SYMBOL')])"`

## Existing Inconsistencies

The largest mismatches between the paper and ArkLib are structural rather than mathematical.

1. Correlated agreement has both predicate and numeric views.
   ArkLib exposes maintained `δ_ε_correlatedAgreement...` predicates in
   [ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ProximityGap/Basic.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ProximityGap/Basic.lean)
   and numeric `epsPg` / `epsCa` values with exact bridges in
   [Errors.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ProximityGap/Errors.lean).

2. General MCA is a first-class generator-level value.
   The canonical definition is `CoreDefinitions.mcaError` in
   [ProximityGenerators.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ProximityGap/ProximityGenerators.lean).
   `epsMca` in [Errors.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ProximityGap/Errors.lean) is transparent
   notation for its affine-line specialization.
3. Some core BCIKS20 interfaces are present, but the list-decoding regime branch is incomplete.
   In particular,
   [ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ProximityGap/BCIKS20/AffineLines/Main.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ProximityGap/BCIKS20/AffineLines/Main.lean)
   still leaves the non-unique-decoding branch as `sorry`.

4. Some "present" proximity-gap files are still proof-incomplete.
   This now means [ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ProximityGap/BCIKS20](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ProximityGap/BCIKS20)
   specifically, which still carries ten `sorry` sites across `WeightedAgreement.lean`,
   `Curves.lean`, `ListDecoding/`, and `AffineLines/Main.lean`.
   [AHIV22.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ProximityGap/AHIV22.lean) and
   `AHIV22Support.lean` are `sorry`-free and are no longer examples of this.

5. ~~Several code families used centrally by the paper are absent.~~ **Resolved.**
   Folded Reed-Solomon ([ReedSolomon/Folded.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ReedSolomon/Folded.lean)),
   univariate multiplicity codes ([ReedSolomon/Multiplicity.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ReedSolomon/Multiplicity.lean)),
   subspace-design codes ([SubspaceDesign.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/SubspaceDesign.lean)),
   and extension-field codes at both encoder and image level
   ([ExtensionCodes.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ExtensionCodes.lean)) are now
   represented. Their list-decoding and MCA bounds are catalogued in the §3–§5 rows above;
   externally sourced results remain marked `present-but-incomplete` until their proofs are
   formalized.

6. The code families deviate from the paper where the paper is defective.
   `ReedSolomon.Folded.Admissible` is **strictly stronger** than ABF26 Definition 2.14: the
   paper quantifies only over distinct pairs `\{\alpha,\beta\} \in \binom{L}{2}`, so its literal
   reading admits `\omega = 1` and permits `0 \in L`. Both are genuine defects — with
   `\omega = 1` the folded-RS distance formula fails (brute-forced), and with `0 \in L`
   Theorem 2.18 is false even given the `\omega`-order hypothesis (compiled counterexample over
   `ZMod 5`). The added intra-orbit clause rules both out, and is therefore **load-bearing for
   Theorem 2.18**, not a stylistic strengthening. Consequence for readers: every downstream
   theorem here is *weaker* than ABF26's printed claim, because it assumes more of `\omega` and
   `L`. See [`../papers/GR08.md`](../papers/GR08.md) and
   [`../papers/ABF26.md`](../papers/ABF26.md) § *Known Divergences From ArkLib*.
