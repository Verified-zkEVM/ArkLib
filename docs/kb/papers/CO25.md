# CO25

## At A Glance

The paper studies a Fiat--Shamir transformation using an overwrite-mode duplex sponge backed
by one ideal permutation and its inverse. ArkLib formalizes the transformation and its
perfect-completeness guarantee for the underlying public-coin interactive proof.

The proof has two separate hybrid arguments that reuse the indices `Hyb₀`--`Hyb₄` for different
experiments: Section 5's main reduction lemma, which gives soundness and knowledge soundness,
and Section 7's chain, which gives zero knowledge.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- The executable absorb/squeeze algorithm and the salted prover transformation.
- An ideal public oracle made of a random hash table and one uniformly random permutation.
- The deferred permutation program for the zero-knowledge simulator and the Figure 5 experiments.

## The Proof

Numbered Lean references follow the authors' revised source. The earlier public ePrint text
labels the duplex-sponge transformation Construction 4.3.

Throughout, `q = |U|` is the alphabet size, `c` the capacity, `r` the rate, `δ` the salt length, and
`L = ⌈δ/r⌉ + L_P + L_V` (`L_totalRateBlocks`) the honest rate-block budget, salt blocks included.

### Completeness

`duplex_sponge_fiat_shamir_perfect_completeness` transfers perfect completeness of the interactive proof,
for a stateless ambient handler and any salt sampler over the ambient oracle.

### Trace and translation procedures

The Section 5 procedures record the query and answer trace, reconstruct chains with
BackTrack and LookAhead, and translate DSFS oracle queries and adversary execution into
the basic Fiat–Shamir experiment. Their Lean implementations live under
`Security/Proofs/TraceTransform.lean` and `Security/Proofs/ProverTransform/`.

### Zero-knowledge hybrids

The simulator installs forward permutation edges so that the sponge squeezes the simulated
verifier messages. Hybrid 1 and Hybrid 2 have equal distributions. The next transition costs
the sum of the codec biases, and the transition from Hybrid 3 to Hybrid 4 costs the honest-verifier
zero-knowledge error. Hybrid 4 is the simulated adaptive NARG experiment. The composition takes
the Hybrid 0 to Hybrid 1 bound as a hypothesis.

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

- **Ambient oracles are supported.** The paper has no ambient oracle. Lean's DSFS perfect-completeness
  theorem allows an ambient oracle specification with a stateless handler.

## Not Formalized

- Soundness, knowledge soundness, and zero knowledge of the duplex-sponge transformation.
- Polynomial running time, and rewinding extractors.
