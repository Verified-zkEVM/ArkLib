# GW13

## At A Glance

`GW13` is Guruswami–Wang, *Linear-Algebraic List Decoding for Variants of Reed–Solomon Codes*,
IEEE Transactions on Information Theory **59**(6) (2013) 3257–3268.
It develops the linear-algebraic (higher-order / "folded and derivative") list-decoding method for
folded Reed–Solomon and univariate multiplicity codes, in which the decoder's solution space is an
affine subspace that is then pruned — the setting in which subspace designs
([`GX13.md`](GX13.md), [`GK16.md`](GK16.md)) became useful.

For ArkLib it is one of the two attribution keys on **univariate multiplicity codes**: `ABF26`
Definition A.7 is tagged `[GW13, KSY14]`, and `ReedSolomon.Multiplicity.umCode` transcribes it.
The companion key is [`KSY14.md`](KSY14.md).

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- Only the **definition** of the univariate multiplicity code, as restated in `ABF26`
  Definitions A.6 and A.7: encode `f̂ ∈ F[X]_{<k}` at each evaluation point by the tuple of its
  first `s` iterated derivatives. Formalized as `ReedSolomon.Multiplicity.umEvalOnPoints` and
  `umCode`.
- No GW13 theorem is formalized. Its list-decoding guarantees, the affine-solution-space
  argument, and its rate/list-size trade-offs are not in ArkLib.

## Version Notes

- The BibTeX entry is the journal version (IEEE TIT 59(6), 2013, DOI `10.1109/TIT.2013.2246813`).
  The reference copy in `~/abf26-refs/GuruswamiW13.pdf` is the earlier preprint, *Electronic
  Colloquium on Computational Complexity*, Report No. 73 (2012); statement numbering may differ
  between the two.
- ArkLib cites `GW13` and `KSY14` **jointly**, exactly as `ABF26` Definition A.7 does, and never
  pins a specific GW13 theorem number. No ArkLib statement therefore depends on which version is
  consulted.

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

- **Ordinary derivatives, not Hasse derivatives.** `ABF26` Definition A.6 specifies the *ordinary*
  formal derivative (`f̂′(X) = Σ_{i≥1} i·aᵢ·X^{i−1}`, iterated), under the global side condition
  `char F ≥ k`. ArkLib transcribes exactly that, with `Polynomial.derivative^[j]`. This is the
  faithful transcription of the source ArkLib is formalizing — it is **not** a small-characteristic
  bug, and it is **not** a duplication of Mathlib's `Polynomial.hasseDeriv`.
- The `char F ≥ k` condition is documented in `Multiplicity.lean` rather than baked into the
  definition, on the paper's own justification (the coefficients `i·aᵢ` do not vanish below degree
  `k`). Any future ArkLib work that needs small characteristic must switch to Hasse derivatives
  and re-derive the degree/multiplicity facts; the ordinary-derivative transcription is licensed
  only by that side condition.
- ArkLib models the code as the image submodule, indexed by an abstract `domain : ι ↪ F`, rather
  than by the paper's concrete evaluation-point sets.
- No ArkLib statement is stronger than anything in GW13; the module is definitional.

## Open Formalization Gaps

- **Dimension and minimum distance of `umCode` are not proved.** Both are stated in the
  literature; neither exists in ArkLib, and `umCode` currently has no in-repo consumers.
- The **multiplicity half of `ABF26` Theorem 2.18 / `GK16` Theorem 14** is deferred: the code is
  defined but the multiplicity-Wronskian argument is not formalized, so the subspace-design
  theorem is proved for folded Reed–Solomon only. (A stale note in `SubspaceDesign.lean` still
  says the derivative operation itself is missing — it is not.)
- GW13's list-decoding results, and the affine-space-plus-subspace-design pruning that connects
  them to `GK16`, are entirely unformalized.
