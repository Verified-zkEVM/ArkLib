# JM24

## At A Glance

`JM24` relates proofs in the algebraic group model to the generic group model. ArkLib has no
algebraic group model of its own: VCVio's `VCVio/CryptoFoundations/GroupModel/` provides it.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- Context for how algebraic-group-model proofs may relate to more generic computation models.

## Main ArkLib Touchpoints

- No ArkLib file cites `JM24`. VCVio's group-model tracking issue
  ([VCVio#846](https://github.com/Verified-zkEVM/VCVio/issues/846)) names it as the source of the
  transfer from algebraic to generic adversaries.

## Version Notes

- ArkLib currently uses `JM24` as a modeling reference, not as a line-by-line theorem target.

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

- ArkLib has no group-oracle model, group representation, or adversary execution for the AGM.
  Generic and algebraic group models are developed in VCVio
  (`VCVio/CryptoFoundations/GroupModel/`).

## Open Formalization Gaps

- KZG binding against generic adversaries, from the reduction inequalities in
  `ArkLib/Commitments/Functional/KZG/` and VCVio's generic `t`-SDH and ARSDH bounds
  ([#1310](https://github.com/Verified-zkEVM/ArkLib/issues/1310)).
