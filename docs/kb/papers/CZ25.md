# CZ25

## At A Glance

`CZ25` is Chen–Zhang, *Explicit folded Reed-Solomon and multiplicity codes achieve relaxed
generalized Singleton bounds*, STOC 2025 — the source of list decodability up to capacity for folded
Reed-Solomon and univariate multiplicity codes, and the engine behind [ABF26] Theorem 3.4 and
Corollary 3.5.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

Three statements, at different levels of generality.

* **Theorem B.5** (Appendix B), the abstract one: a `F`-linear `C ⊆ (F^s)^n` of rate `R = k/sn` which
  is `(ℓ, ℓ(k−1)/(s−ℓ+1))`-strong-subspace-designable for all `ℓ ≤ s` is
  `(L/(L+1)·(1 − sR/(s−L+1)), L)` average-radius list-decodable for any `L ≤ s`.
* **Theorem 1.3 = Theorem 2.20**, the same conclusion for folded Reed-Solomon directly, under `q > n`,
  `s ≥ L` and a generator `γ` of `F_q^×` with "appropriate" evaluation points.
* **Corollary 2.21**, the `(1 − R − ε, L)` capacity form. [ABF26] Corollary 3.5 attributes its display
  to this, though the display is the `η`-form of Theorem 1.3.

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

**The design level is load-bearing: `(k−1)`, not `k`.** [CZ25]'s premise is at `ℓ(k−1)/(s−ℓ+1)`, which
in ArkLib's averaged normalization is the profile `τ(ℓ) = (sR − 1/n)/(s−ℓ+1)`. Read one notch weaker —
at [ABF26] Theorem 2.18's printed `sR/(s−ℓ+1)`, the `k` level — **Theorem B.5 becomes false**. Their
Lemma B.4 derives `∑ᵢ dim(Hᵢ ∩ W) ≥ ℓk/(s−ℓ+1)` and concludes "this contradicts the assumption that
`C` is a `(ℓ, ℓ(k−1)/(s−ℓ+1))`-strong subspace designable code"; at the `k` level the derived bound and
the premise coincide and there is no contradiction to derive.

A compiled axiom-clean counterexample: over `𝔽₂` with `ι = Fin 3`, `s = 1`, `C = span {(1,1,0)}` the
`k`-level condition holds *with equality* while `Λ(C, 1/3) = 2 > 1 = L`. An exhaustive `𝔽₂` sweep finds
385 such code/`L` pairs at `(s,n) ∈ {(1,3),(1,4),(2,3)}`, every one of which satisfies the `k` level
and fails the `(k−1)` level.

Two further divergences: the source proves *average-radius* list decodability, of which the plain `Λ`
bound is a consequence (the safe direction); and [CZ25]'s "strong subspace design" is [GK16]
Definition 3, an absolute bound over subspaces of the message space, whereas `IsSubspaceDesign`
follows [GX13]/[ABF26] in averaging over positions — equivalent through the encoder isomorphism.

## Open Formalization Gaps

The source's stronger average-radius formulation of Theorem B.5. Its plain-list consequence and
Corollary 2.21's capacity form are proved in-tree.
