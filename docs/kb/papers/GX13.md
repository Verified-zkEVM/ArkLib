# GX13

## At A Glance

`GX13` is Guruswami–Xing, STOC '13, pages 843–852 — the paper that **introduced subspace
designs**, as the combinatorial device for pruning the affine solution space produced by
linear-algebraic list decoding.

ArkLib cites it purely for that definitional lineage: it is the attribution attached to
`ABF26` Definition 2.16 and hence to `CodingTheory.IsSubspaceDesign`. No GX13 theorem is
formalized.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- The **original subspace-design definition**: a collection `{H₁, …, H_M}` of subspaces of
  `F_q^m` such that no low-dimensional `W ⊆ F_q^m` has `Σᵢ dim(W ∩ Hᵢ)` too large. This is the
  notion `ABF26` Definition 2.16 recasts and `CodingTheory.IsSubspaceDesign` implements.
- Nothing else. The explicit constructions ArkLib actually reasons about come from `GK16`, and
  the rate bound from `GG25`.

## Version Notes

- **Upstream (ABF26) bibliography defect — ArkLib's own bib entry is correct.** The
  `GuruswamiX13` entry in **ABF26's** `references.bib` (ef-millenium) pairs Guruswami–Xing's
  author/venue/page fields (STOC '13, 843–852) with the *title* of a different paper —
  *Combinatorial list-decoding of Reed–Solomon codes beyond the Johnson radius*
  (Shangguan–Tamo, STOC 2020). The correct title is *List decoding Reed–Solomon,
  Algebraic-Geometric, and Gabidulin subcodes up to the Singleton bound* (verified against the
  title page of the reference copy in `~/abf26-refs/GuruswamiX13.pdf`). ArkLib's
  `blueprint/src/references.bib` `GX13` entry already carries the correct title — no local fix
  needed; report the mix-up to the ABF26 authors alongside the other upstream findings.
- No published-journal variant is tracked; cite the STOC version.

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

- **Formulation side: message space vs code side.** GX13 (and `GK16`) state subspace designs on
  the ambient/message space — subspaces `Hᵢ` of `F_q^m`, intersected with a low-dimensional
  `W ⊆ F_q^m`. ArkLib's `IsSubspaceDesign` instead follows `ABF26` Definition 2.16's **code-side
  recast**: for `A ≤ C ≤ (ι → Fin s → F)` with `dim A ≤ r`, it bounds
  `(Σ_{i} dim Aᵢ)/n ≤ dim A · τ(r)` where `Aᵢ := A ⊓ ker (LinearMap.proj i)`.
- This is not a mismatch, and nothing downstream is wrong: the message-side lift is performed
  inside the `isSubspaceDesign_frsCode` proof itself (`B := degreeLT ⊓ comap enc A`,
  `N i := B ⊓ comap enc (ker (proj i))`), and both steps are dimension-preserving via encoder
  injectivity. But a reader coming from GX13 or GK16 should expect the code-side shape in Lean.
- Consequently, a future ArkLib development that needs the *GX13-shaped* notion (a family of
  subspaces of a bare vector space, with no code attached) will need a second definition; it
  would not be a duplicate of `IsSubspaceDesign` but the ambient-space sibling of it.
- ArkLib's `τ` is indexed over `ℕ` and evaluated at `r ∈ Finset.Icc 1 s`, following ABF26's `[s]`
  rather than GX13's parameterisation.

## Open Formalization Gaps

- No GX13 result is formalized: neither its explicit subspace-design constructions nor the
  linear-algebraic list-decoding application (Reed–Solomon / algebraic-geometric / Gabidulin
  subcodes list-decodable up to the Singleton bound) has an ArkLib counterpart.
- The ambient-space form of the definition, which is what makes GX13's own statements
  expressible, does not exist in ArkLib.
- A bridge lemma between the ambient-space and code-side forms would let the two literatures be
  cited interchangeably; today the translation is re-done by hand inside one proof.
