# Jo26

## At A Glance

`Jo26` proves interleaving stability for two coding-theoretic soundness properties used in modern
IOP/SNARK analyses: generator mutual correlated agreement and curve decodability.
ArkLib proves its exact MCA error transfer for any generator whose seed space has at most as
many elements as the field. The affine-line result is a specialization.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- The exact-transfer case for `|Ω| ≤ q`: any finite nonempty row-wise interleaving preserves
  MCA error for a generator with at most `q` seeds, at every real radius. The forward inequality
  also covers an empty row index.

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

- ArkLib's canonical generator-level value is `CoreDefinitions.mcaError`; affine-line results
  specialize its generator to `AffineLineGenerator F`.
- Curve decodability is not yet a first-class ArkLib coding-theory abstraction.
- The canonical transfer is
  `CoreDefinitions.mcaError_moduleInterleavedCode_eq_of_card_le`. Its forward inequality,
  `mcaError_moduleInterleavedCode_le_of_card_le`, needs no nonempty row index; its reverse
  inequality, `mcaError_le_mcaError_moduleInterleavedCode`, needs no finite row index or
  field-size bound. The affine-line wrappers `ProximityGap.mcaError_interleaved_le`,
  `mcaError_le_moduleInterleavedCode`, and `mcaError_interleaved_eq` retain their old
  hypotheses for callers, although the generic results need no radius guards.

## Open Formalization Gaps

- Formalize the field-size-weighted generator-MCA transfer bound when `|Ω| > q`.
- Introduce curve-decodability and marked curve-decodability definitions.
- Prove the equivalence and interleaving-transfer results for curve decodability.
