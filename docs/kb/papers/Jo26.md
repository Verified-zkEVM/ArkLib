# Jo26

## At A Glance

`Jo26` proves interleaving stability for two coding-theoretic soundness properties used in modern
IOP/SNARK analyses: generator mutual correlated agreement and curve decodability.
ArkLib uses its affine-line result to show that row-wise interleaving preserves MCA error.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- The exact-transfer case for `|Ω| ≤ q`, which makes affine-line MCA invariant under row-wise
  interleaving.

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

- ArkLib's canonical generator-level value is `CoreDefinitions.mcaError`; affine-line results
  specialize its generator to `AffineLineGenerator F`.
- Curve decodability is not yet a first-class ArkLib coding-theory abstraction.
- The affine-line exact-transfer corollary is present directly on `mcaError` with the source's
  nonempty-interleaving and open-radius guards. The two inequalities are
  `ProximityGap.mcaError_interleaved_le` and `mcaError_le_moduleInterleavedCode`;
  `mcaError_interleaved_eq` combines them by antisymmetry.

## Open Formalization Gaps

- Formalize the general field-size-weighted generator-MCA transfer bound.
- Introduce curve-decodability and marked curve-decodability definitions.
- Prove the equivalence and interleaving-transfer results for curve decodability.
