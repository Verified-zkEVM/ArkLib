# MS77

## At A Glance

`MS77` is MacWilliams–Sloane, *The Theory of Error-Correcting Codes*, North-Holland (1977) — the
standard reference, cited by [ABF26] for the single-term lower bound on Hamming-ball volume.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

The estimate `Vol_q(δ, n) ≥ q^{n·H_q(δ)} / √(8·n·δ·(1−δ))`, which turns the [Eli57] volume bound into
[ABF26] Corollary 3.8's entropy form.

## Main ArkLib Touchpoints

`linear_lambda_ge_entropy_volume` in [Bounds.lean](../../../ArkLib/Data/CodingTheory/ListDecodability/Bounds.lean) proves exactly this estimate in-tree;
`CodingTheory.hammingBallVolume` and `CodingTheory.qEntropy` are the support layer.

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

The estimate is classically stated for the binary case. It reduces to it exactly: keeping only the top
binomial term, `Vol_q(δ,n) ≥ C(n, δn)·(q−1)^{δn}`, and `q^{n·H_q(δ)} = (q−1)^{δn}·2^{n·h₂(δ)}`
identically, so the `q`-ary claim *is* the classical `C(n,d) ≥ 2^{n·h₂(d/n)}/√(8n·(d/n)(1−d/n))`. That
bound is symmetric under `d ↦ n−d`, so no `δ ≤ 1−1/q` restriction is needed.

`δ·n` must be an integer, and this is not decoration: at `ρ = 1`, `δ = 1/(2n)` the right-hand side
exceeds `1` while the relative ball collapses to radius `0` and `Λ = 1`.

## Open Formalization Gaps

The sharper refinements from [DG25dist]. The explicit single-term estimate used by [ABF26] is
proved in-tree through Mathlib's finite Stirling-sequence bounds.
