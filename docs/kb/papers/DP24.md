# DP24

## At A Glance

`DP24` is the current binary-tower multilinear-proof reference cited in ArkLib's Binius
development.
It is the main paper page to consult when reviewing or extending the `ProofSystem/Binius` subtree.

DP24's **ring-switching** reduction has been factored out of the Binius subtree into
`ProofSystem/RingSwitching/Packing/`, parameterized over the shared packing-algebra
layer `RingSwitchingProfile` (`Packing/Profile.lean`), with Binius as its instance.
It is one construction of the ring-switching family — see the taxonomy umbrella
`ProofSystem/RingSwitching/Basic.lean`. See also the
concept page [`../concepts/ring-switching.md`](../concepts/ring-switching.md) and the blueprint
section *Ring Switching* (`blueprint/src/proof_systems/ring_switching.tex`).

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- High-level problem framing and protocol lineage for Binius-related developments.
- Reference context for the binary-tower multilinear proof system components in ArkLib.
- The **ring-switching reduction** (§2.5 eq̃/trace identity; §3.1–3.2 soundness errors): packing a
  small-field multilinear into a large-field one and reducing the evaluation claim. The §2.5 identity
  is formalized as the reconstruction and inverse laws of `RingSwitchingProfile`
  (`decomposeRows_spec`, `decomposeColumns_spec`, `decomposeRows_recompose`,
  `decomposeColumns_recompose`, `embeddings_agree`), proven for the Binius (tensor-algebra)
  instance.

- [`ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/FRIBinius/Prelude.lean`](../../../ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/FRIBinius/Prelude.lean) — `biniusProfile`, the concrete Binius instantiation.
- [`ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/Spec.lean`](../../../ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/Spec.lean) — the Binary Basefold protocol specification.
- [`ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/Soundness/Incremental.lean`](../../../ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/Soundness/Incremental.lean) — `prob_incrementalFoldingBadEvent_fresh_le`, the per-challenge bound behind the fold step's soundness. Summed over the `ϑ` challenges of a block it gives Proposition 4.21; the per-challenge form is not in the paper (an earlier draft of the port, #383, called it "Proposition 4.21.2", its incremental variant of Proposition 4.21). Lemma 4.22 is `preTensorCombine_not_jointProximityNat_of_not_fiberwiseClose` (`Soundness/Lift.lean`).

- [`ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/ReductionLogic.lean`](../../../ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/ReductionLogic.lean) — the logic of the single steps of the evaluation IOP of Construction 4.12: the fold step (lines 1–4 of the loop), the commit step (line 6) and the final sum-check step (line 5 and step 3), with their strong completeness, the per-step part of Theorem 4.13.
- [`ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/Steps/Fold.lean`](../../../ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/Steps/Fold.lean) — worst-case round-by-round knowledge soundness of the fold step, `foldOracleVerifier_rbrKnowledgeSoundnessWorstCaseWith`, with error `2 / |L|` (the sum-check round) plus the bound of `prob_incrementalFoldingBadEvent_fresh_le` (the per-challenge form of the folding bad event of Definition 4.20 and Proposition 4.21, used in the proof of Theorem 4.17). The commit, relay and final sum-check steps (`Steps/{Commit,Relay,FinalSumcheck}.lean`) send no challenge; their soundness content is their knowledge-state functions. The relay step, which moves the oracle statements to the next round outside a commitment round, is a formalization device with no counterpart in the paper.

These are the current paper-entry modules for the Binius line of work. The Binary Basefold core
interaction, query phase and full protocol, and the FRI-Binius core interaction and full protocol,
are retired pending the rest of the port of #383 (tracking issue #1286).

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

- ArkLib currently uses `DP24` primarily as a protocol-family reference rather than as a complete
  one-to-one theorem map.
- Version drift across `DP23`/`DP24`/`DP25` should be tracked explicitly when proof obligations
  depend on exact paper statements.
- Binius statements assume `ℓ + 𝓡 < r` (strict), whereas [DP24] §4.1 allows `R ≤ r − ℓ`, i.e.
  `ℓ + R ≤ r`. The constraint comes from CompPoly's `Fin r`-indexed subspaces `U` (the evaluation
  domains `AdditiveNTT.sDomain` are images of `U 𝔽q β ⟨ℓ + R, _⟩`), so the case `ℓ + R = r` is
  not covered; for instance, the bound in `Soundness/Incremental.lean` recovers Proposition 4.21
  only for `ℓ + R < r`.
