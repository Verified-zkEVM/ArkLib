# DP24

## At A Glance

`DP24` is the current binary-tower multilinear-proof reference cited in ArkLib's Binius
development.
It is the main paper page to consult when reviewing or extending the `ProofSystem/Binius` subtree.

DP24's **ring-switching** reduction has been factored out of the Binius subtree into
`ProofSystem/RingSwitching/Packing/`, parameterized over the shared packing-algebra
layer `RingSwitchingProfile` (`Packing/Profile.lean`). Binius instantiates the carrier and
reuses batching, then interleaves FRI and sumcheck in its own downstream pipeline.
It is one construction of the ring-switching family — see the taxonomy umbrella
`ProofSystem/RingSwitching/Basic.lean`. See also the
concept page [`../concepts/ring-switching.md`](../concepts/ring-switching.md) and the blueprint
section *Ring Switching* (`blueprint/src/proof_systems/ring_switching.tex`).

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- High-level problem framing and protocol lineage for Binius-related developments.
- Reference context for the binary-tower multilinear proof system components in ArkLib.
- The **ring-switching reduction** (§2.5 tensor-product packing; Construction 3.1 and
  Theorem 3.5): packing a small-field multilinear into a large-field one and reducing the
  evaluation claim. The tensor coordinates must agree with packing and evaluation. The
  repaired profile now has two-sided coordinate inverse laws and coherent base embeddings.
  Its native batching head now consumes the shared finite observation, scalar reconstruction
  and commitment-relation separation proofs. State-aware completeness and exact worst-case
  knowledge security are proved, and FRI-Binius supplies its actual unique-distance binding.
  The final multiplier identity and final-leaf completeness/knowledge are also proved; legacy
  loop and unrestricted general-composition admissions still limit the full legacy pipeline.
  See the [model and coverage audit](../audits/ring-switching-model-coverage.md).

## Open Formalization Gaps

- Close the legacy loop and unrestricted general-composition admissions. The native batching
  head is proved and consumed by the actual FRI-Binius completeness assembly; that full assembly
  still depends on downstream admissions. Its one tensor message and vector challenge are
  preserved. The independent `ScalarOpening` construction composes DP24's concrete scalar layout
  with the generic family and sumcheck tail, proving completeness and exact round-by-round
  knowledge security on the packed opening relation. That generic composition has a redundant
  checked-slice message. The actual interleaved FRI-Binius downstream opening remains separate.
  Its final verifier's absorbing rejection and value forwarding have local execution proofs and
  GF(16) regressions, which do not discharge its downstream security obligations.
  See the [coverage audit](../audits/ring-switching-model-coverage.md).
- Pin the exact PDF revision when using Construction 3.1 / Theorem 3.5: the audit inspects a
  50-page PDF with 2025-09-22 metadata, while the ePrint landing page reports a 2026-05-14
  revision. The legacy additional final-step `1/|L|` term is not a separate random challenge
  in the source protocol.
