# DP24

## At A Glance

`DP24` is the current binary-tower multilinear-proof reference cited in ArkLib's Binius
development.
It is the main paper page to consult when reviewing or extending the `ProofSystem/Binius` subtree.

DP24's **ring-switching** reduction has been factored out of the Binius subtree into
`ProofSystem/RingSwitching/Packing/`, parameterized over the shared packing-algebra
layer `PackingData`; `TensorPrefix` specializes the shared `RingSwitch` protocol to
the tensor carrier and original prefix layout, with Binius as its concrete consumer.
It is one construction of the ring-switching family — see the taxonomy umbrella
`ProofSystem/RingSwitching/Basic.lean`. See also the
concept page [`../concepts/ring-switching.md`](../concepts/ring-switching.md) and the blueprint
section *Ring Switching* (`blueprint/src/proof_systems/ring_switching.tex`).

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- High-level problem framing and protocol lineage for Binius-related developments.
- Reference context for the binary-tower multilinear proof system components in ArkLib.
- The **ring-switching reduction** (§2.5 eq̃/trace identity; §3.1–3.2 soundness errors): packing a
  small-field multilinear into a large-field one and reducing the evaluation claim. The §2.5 identity
  is formalized as the reconstruction and inverse laws of `RingSwitchingProfile`
  (`decomposeRows_spec`, `decomposeColumns_spec`, `decomposeRows_recompose`,
  `decomposeColumns_recompose`, `embeddings_agree`), proven for the Binius (tensor-algebra)
  instance.

This is the per-iteration bound in the proof of Proposition 4.24, stated for a fixed statement and fixed oracles, with the paper's standing assumption that none of `E₀, …, E_{ℓ−ϑ}` occurs as a hypothesis; the query phase raises it to the `γ` repetitions. Lemma 4.25 is `not_pairUniqueDecodingClose_iteratedFold_of_not_isCompliant` (`Soundness/FoldDistance.lean`) and Lemma 4.26 is `not_logicalCheckSingleRepetition_of_mem_disagreementSet` (`Soundness/QueryRejection.lean`). - [`ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/Steps/Fold.lean`](../../../ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/Steps/Fold.lean) — round-by-round knowledge soundness of the fold step, `foldOracleVerifier_rbrKnowledgeSoundnessWith`, with error `2 / |L|` (the sum-check round) plus the bound of `prob_incrementalFoldingBadEvent_fresh_le` (the per-challenge form of the folding bad event of Definition 4.20 and Proposition 4.21, used in the proof of Theorem 4.17). The commit, relay and final sum-check steps (`Steps/{Commit,Relay,FinalSumcheck}.lean`) send no challenge; their soundness content is their knowledge-state functions. The relay step, which moves the oracle statements to the next round outside a commitment round, is a formalization device with no counterpart in the paper.

- [`ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/CoreInteractionPhase.lean`](../../../ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/CoreInteractionPhase.lean) — the core interaction, steps 1–3 of the evaluation IOP of Construction 4.12, with perfect completeness and round-by-round knowledge soundness composed from the steps' (`CoreInteractionPhase/{Protocol,Security}.lean` hold the rounds and blocks of the loop). `sumcheckFoldKnowledgeError_le` bounds the total error of the loop by `2ℓ / |L| + 2^(ℓ + 𝓡) / |L|`: the Schwartz–Zippel terms of the `ℓ` rounds, and the fold bad-event terms, whose sum is the expression bounded in the proof of Proposition 4.23.

- [`ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/QueryPhase.lean`](../../../ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/QueryPhase.lean) — the oracle-query prover and verifier, exact acceptance and sampled perfect completeness under the strict honestly encoded input relation, and RBR soundness under the relaxed final relation. Its soundness proof connects successful stateful execution to the pure proximity checks before applying the repeated-query bound.
- [`ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/General.lean`](../../../ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/BinaryBasefold/General.lean) — the core-and-query protocol, strict-input perfect completeness, relaxed-input RBR soundness, and the total error bound `2ℓ/|L| + 2^(ℓ+𝓡)/|L| + (1/2 + 1/(2·2^𝓡))^γ`.

- [`ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/FRIBinius/Unrolled/General.lean`](../../../ArkLib/ProofSystem/Binius/FRIBinius/Unrolled/General.lean) — Construction 5.1 for `K = GF(2)`. Steps 1–3 and 5 are the tensor batching head `TensorPrefix.headVerifier`: its check is DP24's step 2 and its output target is step 5's `s₀` (`TensorPrefix.headVerifier_eq`); step 4 is prover-only. Steps 6–9 are the Binary Basefold opening of that product claim at the ring-switching multiplier `TensorPrefix.multiplier` (`ProductClaim.openingVerifier`). The check of step 8 is `s_ℓ' = A(r') · c`, where the verifier's value of `A(r')` equals the value `compute_final_eq_value` of steps 7–8 (`TensorPrefix.multiplier_evaluate_eq_compute_final_eq_value`). With `K = GF(2)`, DP24's degree `r` of `L` over `𝔽₂` is the basis size `2^κ`; the statements need `ℓ' + R < 2^κ`, where Construction 5.1 asks for `r ≥ ℓ + R`: only the packed polynomial is encoded. Construction 5.1 states the folding-factor condition as `ϑ ∣ ℓ`, while §5.2 and the code use `ϑ ∣ ℓ'`, the number of packed variables that are folded. `Unrolled.fullError_sum_le_concrete` bounds the challenge errors by equation (43), and `Unrolled.concreteError_add_eq` relates it to the bound of the composition with a separate ring-switching sum-check.

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

- ArkLib currently uses `DP24` primarily as a protocol-family reference rather than as a complete
  one-to-one theorem map.
- Version drift across `DP23`/`DP24`/`DP25` should be tracked explicitly when proof obligations
  depend on exact paper statements.
- Binius statements assume `ℓ + 𝓡 < r` (strict), whereas [DP24] §4.1 allows `R ≤ r − ℓ`, i.e.
  `ℓ + R ≤ r`. The constraint comes from CompPoly's `Fin r`-indexed subspaces `U` (the evaluation
  domains `AdditiveNTT.sDomain` are images of `U 𝔽q β ⟨ℓ + R, _⟩`), so the case `ℓ + R = r` is
  not covered; for instance, the bound in `Soundness/Incremental.lean` recovers Proposition 4.21
  only for `ℓ + R < r`.
- The ring-switching compositions (`FRIBinius/RingSwitch`, `FRIBinius/Unrolled`) fix `K = GF(2)`;
  Construction 5.1 allows any binary `K`. They use one basis `β : Basis (Fin (2^κ)) K L` both for
  packing (Definition 2.2, any `K`-basis) and for the additive NTT (§4.1, an `𝔽₂`-basis with
  `β₀ = 1`), so the packing basis must also satisfy `β 0 = 1`.
- The unrolled protocol's rate condition is `ℓ' + R < 2^κ`, where Construction 5.1 asks for
  `r ≥ ℓ + R`: only the packed polynomial is encoded, and the inequality is strict as above. Every
  parameter set Construction 5.1 allows for `K = 𝔽₂` with `ℓ' ≥ 1`, as `[NeZero ℓ']` requires,
  satisfies it. Construction 5.1 writes the
  folding condition as `ϑ ∣ ℓ`; §5.2 and the code use `ϑ ∣ ℓ'`.
- Security notion. Definition 2.9 asks for an efficient emulator that works from the commitment
  alone, and the verifier samples the evaluation point after the commitment. ArkLib's theorems fix
  the point in the input statement, quantify over every point, and use an unbounded extractor that
  also sees the point and the claimed value (`Verifier.knowledgeSoundness`, which is relation
  soundness by `Verifier.knowledgeSoundness_iff_relationSoundness`). The input relation is
  `s = t(r)` together with unique-decoding-radius closeness of the committed oracle to the encoding
  of the packed `t`; within that radius Algorithm 1 returns the same polynomial, matching the
  paper's `t ≠ ⊥ ∧ t(r) = s` (Lemma 2.1, Remark 4.27). The extractor decodes the first oracle with
  Berlekamp–Welch at the exact distance (`BinaryBasefold.extractMlp`) rather than at the bound
  `(d − 1)/2`; the two agree inside the unique-decoding radius.
