# DP24

## At A Glance

Diamond and Posen's *Polylogarithmic Proofs for Multilinears over Binary Towers* supplies the
coordinate ring-switching construction used by ArkLib's Binius development. A scalar evaluation
of a base-field multilinear is reduced to an opening of a packed extension-field multilinear.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

Section 2.5 gives the tensor-product representation; Construction 3.1 and Theorem 3.5 give the
reduction and its security analysis. The source table splits into κ packed prefix bits and m
retained suffix bits. A tensor message contains partial evaluations: row coordinates reconstruct
the scalar claim, and column coordinates retain packed values for batching.

`RingSwitchingProfile` supplies faithful coordinates and coherent base embeddings.
`ProfileLayout.lean` identifies profile `packMLE` with the shared DP24 `splitFirst` layout;
`BatchingAlgebra.lean` derives the scalar guard and round-zero residual relation from finite and
checked observation. Tensor batching has state-aware completeness and worst-case-per-prefix
knowledge soundness. FRI-Binius supplies functionality from its actual unique-distance commitment
relation, retains the one-tensor-message/vector-challenge head, and interleaves FRI with sumcheck
in its suffix.

## Implementation Boundary

The standalone generic scalar pipeline sends partial values and then a redundant checked-slice
message. Its completeness and round-by-round knowledge contracts end at the same commitment's
packed evaluation relation. It is a composition variant of the source transcript.

Tensor batching and the profile-based final leaf have proved completeness and knowledge contracts.
The profile-based loop and unrestricted composition retain admissions. FRI-Binius's final verifier
has absorbing rejection and forwards the received packed value, with local execution proofs;
its downstream interleaved security and full completeness assembly remain partly admitted.
The [coverage audit](../audits/ring-switching-model-coverage.md) gives the component bounds and
source correspondence.
