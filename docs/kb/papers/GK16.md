# GK16

## At A Glance

`GK16` is Guruswami–Kopparty, *Explicit subspace designs*, Combinatorica **36**(2) (2016)
161–185.
A subspace design is a collection `{H₁, …, H_M}` of subspaces of `F_q^m` such that no
low-dimensional subspace `W` of `F_q^m` intersects too many members of the collection.
GK16 gives the first explicit constructions, and in particular shows that folded Reed–Solomon
and univariate multiplicity codes give subspace designs.

For ArkLib it is the **proof engine** behind both halves of `ABF26` Theorem 2.18. The folded-RS
half uses the folded Wronskian (Definition 11), its independence criterion (Lemma 12), Theorem 14,
and Appendix A. The univariate-multiplicity half uses the classical Wronskian (Definition 9), its
independence criterion (Lemma 10), and the §5 multiplicity argument culminating in Theorem 17.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- **Definition 11 (folded Wronskian).** For `P₁, …, P_σ ∈ F[X]` and `γ ∈ F*`, the `σ × σ`
  determinant whose row `i` is `(P₁(γⁱX), …, P_σ(γⁱX))`. Transcribed verbatim as
  `Polynomial.foldedWronskian` — rows are the `γ`-twists, columns the polynomials.
- **Lemma 12 (the criterion).** For `m < |F| = q`, `γ` a **generator** of `F*`, and
  `P₁, …, P_σ ∈ F_q[X]_{<m}`: the `Pⱼ` are `F_q`-linearly independent **iff** the folded
  Wronskian is nonzero. ArkLib has both directions
  (`foldedWronskian_ne_zero_of_linearIndependent` for the substantial one, plus the easy
  converse and a packaged `iff`).
- **Definition 9 / Lemma 10 (classical Wronskian).** The determinant of the matrix of ordinary
  iterated derivatives is nonzero on a basis of degree-`< k` polynomials in sufficiently large
  characteristic. Formalized as `Polynomial.classicalWronskian` and the reusable basis-form
  criterion `classicalWronskian_ne_zero_of_basis`.
- **Theorem 14 (the subspace-design theorem)** and its counting identity
  `(m−1)·s ≥ r(t−s+1)·Σ_α dim(W ∩ H_α)`, instantiated at extension-degree parameter `r = 1`,
  the base-field folded-RS setting used by ABF26.
- **§5 / Theorem 17 (the univariate-multiplicity construction).** The classical Wronskian
  acquires root multiplicity `(s−σ+1)·dim Nᵢ` at each evaluation point; comparing their sum to
  its degree yields the UM subspace-design profile. Formalized as
  `CodingTheory.isSubspaceDesign_umCode`.
- **Appendix A**, the proof of Lemma 12: `E(X) = X^{q−1} − γ` is irreducible,
  `X^q ≡ γX`, `Pⱼ(X^{qʲ}) = Pⱼ(X)^{qʲ}`, and a `q`-linearised polynomial
  `Q(Y) = Σ αᵢ Y^{qⁱ}` of degree `≤ q^{σ−1}` vanishing on a space of size `q^σ`.

## Version Notes

- The BibTeX entry is the **journal** version (Combinatorica 36(2), 2016). The reference copy in
  `~/abf26-refs/GuruswamiK16.pdf` is the earlier preprint, titled *Explicit Subspace Designs*
  and dated **March 2013**.
- ArkLib's statement numbers (Definition 11, Lemma 12, Theorem 14, Appendix A) are the
  preprint's. If a numbering discrepancy matters, check against the Combinatorica version
  before editing a docstring — the mathematics quoted in the Lean docstrings has been verified
  against the preprint text.
- The notation map used throughout ArkLib: GK16 `γ` ↦ `ω`, GK16 `m` ↦ `k`, GK16 `s`
  (subspace dimension) ↦ `σ = dim A`, GK16 `t` (fold arity) ↦ `s`, GK16 `r` ↦ `1`.

## Known Divergences From ArkLib

- **Proof route: streamlined, not copied.** GK16's Appendix A clears denominators over `F(X)`
  after extracting a common factor from the `Aᵢ`. ArkLib instead obtains the row dependency
  directly over `K = F[X]/(X^{q−1} − ω)` via `Matrix.exists_vecMul_eq_zero_iff`. The two
  arguments reach the same contradiction (`q^σ ≤ q^{σ−1}`); the Lean route avoids the fraction
  field entirely.
- **ArkLib proves an assertion GK16 leaves unproved.** GK16 says of `X^{q−1} − γ` only that it
  "happens to be irreducible". ArkLib proves it
  (`X_pow_card_sub_one_sub_C_irreducible`: any irreducible factor of degree `d` forces
  `ω^d = 1`, hence `(q−1) ∣ d ≤ q−1`). Mathlib's Kummer-style criteria do not cover the even
  exponent `q−1`, so this is genuine added content rather than a transcription.
- **The generator hypothesis matters, and downstream restatements drop it.** GK16 Lemma 12 says
  "let `γ ∈ F*` be a generator". `ABF26` Theorem 2.18 and `GG25` Theorem 2.19 both restate the
  subspace-design result **without any order condition on `ω`**, and both are false as
  restated: a compiled refutation over `𝔽₁₇` (`ι = Fin 7`, `domain = 1..7`, `s = 2`, `k = 3`,
  `ω = −1`, so `orderOf ω = 2 ≠ 16`) gives `Σ dim Aᵢ / n = 1 > 6/7 = dim A · τ(2)`. ArkLib
  therefore carries `hω_gen : orderOf ω = Fintype.card F − 1`, which is GK16's own hypothesis
  and not an ArkLib invention. See [`GG25.md`](GG25.md) and [`ABF26.md`](ABF26.md).
- **`0 ∈ L` is a downstream-only defect.** ABF26 T2.18 and GG25 Thm 2.19 also permit `0` as an
  evaluation point, under which they are false even with the order condition. GK16 is *not*
  affected: its §4.2 setup requires `F_q(α) = F_{q^r}` with `|S_α| = r·t`, which excludes
  `α = 0`.
- **Formulation side.** GK16 states subspace designs on the ambient space `F_q^m` (collections of
  subspaces `Hᵢ ⊆ F_q^m`); ArkLib's `IsSubspaceDesign` is ABF26's code-side recast. The
  message-side lift is done inside the T2.18 proof (`B := degreeLT ⊓ comap enc A`), so nothing is
  lost — see [`GX13.md`](GX13.md).
- Mathlib's `Polynomial.wronskian` (`a·b′ − a′·b`, used for Mason–Stothers) is the two-polynomial
  classical case. ArkLib's `Polynomial.classicalWronskian` supplies GK16's arbitrary finite
  matrix, while `foldedWronskian` is the distinct twist determinant used by the FRS construction.
- **The UM theorem uses slightly weaker hypotheses than the survey states, without weakening its
  conclusion.** The injection `domain : ι ↪ F` already gives the point distinctness for which
  ABF26 imposes the shared `|F| > |ι|` guard, so that cardinality inequality is unnecessary.
  ArkLib assumes `k ≤ ringChar F`; because messages have degree `< k`, all derivative orders
  that must be units are `< k`, so this also covers the endpoint `k = ringChar F` that the
  source's strict characteristic guard omits.

## Open Formalization Gaps

- Neither half of ABF26 T2.18 is open: the folded-RS and univariate-multiplicity statements are
  both proved. GK16's broader construction and application results beyond these code-side
  theorems remain out of scope.
- ArkLib instantiates GK16's field-extension-degree parameter `r` at `1`, exactly the base-field
  folded-RS setting used by ABF26. This should not be confused with the code-side profile input:
  `isSubspaceDesign_frsCode` proves the design inequality for every input-subspace dimension.
- GK16's other explicit *constructions* and their list-decoding applications (the paper's own headline)
  are out of scope: ArkLib uses GK16 as a proof technique for a code-family property, not as a
  source of constructions.
