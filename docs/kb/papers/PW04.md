# PW04

## At A Glance

Pellikaan and Wu use an extension field of degree `k` to express Reed–Muller evaluations
on a `k`-dimensional grid as Reed–Solomon evaluations in one variable.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

The substitution used in [CWYZ26, Appendix A](CWYZ26.md) realizes the reduction with trace
coordinates. It preserves evaluations and bounds the univariate degree by the total degree
times `q₀^(k - 1)`, where `q₀` is the grid field size.
