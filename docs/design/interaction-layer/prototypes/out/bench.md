The skeleton export costs time exponential in the number of rounds: computing the skeleton builds the list of all `2^n` points of the input claim's domain (`Dom.pow` in `Specs.Formula`). The probe at 32 rounds therefore stops at the time limit, and the bench has no export case at 128 rounds. The accessor at round 127 of the 128-round case needs `maxRecDepth` 1024; Lean's default of 512 suffices up to round 120. See `Specs/Bench.lean`.
