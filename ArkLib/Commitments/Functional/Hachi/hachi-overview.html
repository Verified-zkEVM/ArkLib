The Hachi evaluation opening, seam by seam

Hachi [NOZ26] is a lattice-based multilinear polynomial commitment over the power-of-two cyclotomic ring Z_q[X]/(X^{2^α}+1). The core of one opening is a single line of nine reductions — from the quadratic-form evaluation (QuadEval) to a bare multilinear-evaluation claim on the committed table — followed by three §4.5 adapters (rows 10–12) that hand that claim to the next iteration. This map places every protocol-bearing file on that line, marks the bridges and subfolders, and shows exactly what is proven, repaired, or in progress.

Node types & status keys

On the reduction line

2 Subprotocol step — a numbered link that runs a real interactive round (a message and/or a challenge) and carries a coordinate-wise-special-soundness (CWSS) extractor.
Bridge / adapter bridge — a zero-round link that only reshapes the statement to glue one subprotocol's output relation to the next one's input. Sound for any structure.
guarded Guarded verifier — its runtime check reads data the next statement type drops (a sumcheck target, a packed value); it may failure at runtime and composes via ▷ₑᵍ.

Proof status

proven Sorry-free on the soundness side. Every proven link of the opening line (rows 1–9) is axiom-clean — only propext / Classical.choice / Quot.sound — and they compose that way: openCore, roundsChain and finalEvalPackage carry no sorryAx. The honest-prover / completeness layer is a separate gap, open for the whole chain (see the roadmap), so a proven pill means the CWSS certificate, not the forward direction.
repaired Sorry-free, but deliberately not the paper's claim: the printed statement is false or unprovable as written, and what is proved here is a corrected variant. The theorem is real — the divergence is what this pill flags, so it is not counted as proven. Row 6 (Lemma 10) is the only one: the uniform-vector extraction is refuted by exists_nonzero_vanishing_on_axis_cross and replaced by per-coordinate scalar rounds with a nested binary-evaluation-tree zero test, at the cost of a temporary liftShort assumption. Each such divergence is recorded in docs/kb/audits/.
wip A real skeleton: types, relations, verifier and package are defined and typecheck; the CWSS proof (and sometimes the encoding defs) are sorry. All of it now sits in the §4.5 recursion tail (rows 10 and 12) plus the two packaging files — Composition.lean has no sorries of its own, and Commitment.lean's single sorry is the opening placeholder.
gap A documented soundness gap: the paper's step is (apparently) not knowledge-sound as stated, so the pull-back sorry stands as the record — the §4.5/§3.2 partial-evaluation gap at row 11.
planned Named in the composition roadmap but not yet a file — future heads, tails, and the completeness layer.
The composed opening

The reduction line — QuadEval → evaluation claim → next iteration

Top to bottom is one opening of an Rq-committed multilinear polynomial, exactly as composed in Composition.lean (openingChain). Each link reduces one relation to the next, and every relation is the ordinary protocol relation. Where extraction can fail cryptographically instead of producing a witness (QuadEval's Module-SIS break, the weak-binding collisions of Figures 4–6), the link carries an escape event on the transcript tree (EscapeCWSSPackage.esc): a predicate on (statement, tree) entering the certificate as a disjunct of its conclusion, so no extractor can fabricate it. Row 9 outputs a bare multilinear-evaluation claim mle[w̃](a) = y′ on the committed table; rows 10–12 turn that into the next iteration's QuadEval statement over the next ring Φ′, closing the recursion loop.
