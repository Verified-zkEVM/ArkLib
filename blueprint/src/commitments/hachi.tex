\section{Nonrecursive Hachi}\label{sec:hachi_nonrecursive}

ArkLib formalizes a nonrecursive variant of the Hachi lattice-based multilinear polynomial
commitment~\cite{NOZ26}, which builds on Greyhound~\cite{NS24}. The recursive opening of the
paper, which carries each iteration's evaluation claim to the next ring instead of closing
it, is not formalized. Section~\ref{sec:hachi_subfield} covers the extension-field packing
that the scheme uses.

Throughout, $R_q=\mathbb{Z}_q[X]/(X^{2^\alpha}+1)$. The committed data is a multilinear
polynomial over $R_q$ in $r+m$ variables, and a query is an evaluation point. Honest range
parameters consist of a balanced digit base $b$, a ball radius $\gamma$ and a zero-check
digit base $b_0$, subject to
\[
  1<b\leq q/2,\qquad 1<b_0\leq q/2,\qquad
  \lfloor b/2\rfloor\leq\gamma,\qquad \lfloor b_0/2\rfloor\leq\gamma\leq b_0-1.
\]
The remaining parameters are the challenge weight $\omega$, the digit count $\tau$ and
bound $z_{\max}$ for the folded witness, a sumcheck dimension $M$, and a ring homomorphism
$\mathbb{Z}_q\to\field$ into the field over which the sumcheck runs. Write
$\delta_b=\lceil\log_b q\rceil$, and write $\mu_0$ and $n_0$ for the column and row counts
of the linear relation that the ring-switching step produces.

\begin{definition}[Nonrecursive Hachi]\label{def:hachi_nonrecursive}
\lean{ArkLib.Lattices.Ajtai.InnerOuter.hachiNonrecursive,
ArkLib.Lattices.Ajtai.InnerOuter.hachiNonrecursiveOpening,
ArkLib.Lattices.Ajtai.InnerOuter.hachiNonrecursiveConcrete,
ArkLib.Lattices.Ajtai.InnerOuter.nonrecursiveLiftCom}
\leanok
Key generation samples the public matrices of the inner-outer Ajtai commitment. The committer
decomposes the coefficients in balanced base $b$ and commits with the inner and outer
matrices. The opening proves an evaluation claim through the chain: input adapter, bridge,
quadratic evaluation, ring switching to a linear relation, lift, batching, nested zero-check
and sumcheck. A terminal step closes the chain: the prover sends the lifted witness in the
clear and the verifier checks the reduced evaluation claim on it. The opening is therefore
not succinct. The scheme is generic in the commitment used by the lift step. The concrete
instance commits to the lifted witness with an Ajtai matrix of $\mu_0+n_0\lceil\log_{b_0}q\rceil$
columns, whose quotient block is decomposed in base $b_0$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Perfect correctness of nonrecursive Hachi]\label{thm:hachi_correctness}
\lean{ArkLib.Lattices.Ajtai.InnerOuter.hachiNonrecursive_perfectCorrectness,
ArkLib.Lattices.Ajtai.InnerOuter.hachiNonrecursiveConcrete_perfectCorrectness,
ArkLib.Lattices.Ajtai.InnerOuter.hachiNonrecursiveOpening_perfectCompleteness}
\leanok
\uses{def:hachi_nonrecursive}
Assume
\begin{itemize}
  \item $z_{\max}\leq(b-1-\lfloor b/2\rfloor)\sum_{e<\tau}b^{e}$, the positive capacity of
    $\tau$ balanced base-$b$ digits;
  \item $2^{r}\,\omega\,\lfloor b/2\rfloor\leq z_{\max}$ and $\tau>0$;
  \item $\delta_b>0$, $2^\alpha>0$ and $b_0>0$;
  \item $(\mu_0+n_0\lceil\log_{b_0}q\rceil)\cdot 2^{\alpha}\leq 2^{M+1}$;
  \item $b_0-1\leq\gamma$.
\end{itemize}
Then, for every lift commitment, every initial oracle state and every implementation of the
uniform oracle, the scheme is perfectly correct. The same holds for the concrete instance.
The opening reduction alone is perfectly complete under these assumptions together with
$\kappa\geq 1$ and a bound $\beta^2\geq 2^m\delta_b\,2^\alpha\lfloor b/2\rfloor^2$ on its
auxiliary parameters $\kappa$ and $\beta^2$.
\end{theorem}

With the honest range constraint $\gamma\leq b_0-1$, the last assumption forces
$\gamma=b_0-1$.

\paragraph{Soundness.}
The soundness side is formalized for the reduction chain, not for the scheme. Each
subprotocol carries a coordinate-wise special soundness certificate whose extraction may
instead output an escape event: a Module-SIS solution for the public matrices, or a
collision in the lift commitment.

\begin{theorem}[Special soundness of one Hachi iteration]\label{thm:hachi_iteration_cwss}
\lean{ArkLib.Lattices.Ajtai.InnerOuter.iteration,
ArkLib.Lattices.Ajtai.InnerOuter.hachi_iteration_coordinateWiseSpecialSoundWithEscape,
ArkLib.Lattices.Ajtai.InnerOuter.evaluation}
\leanok
Assume $q\equiv 5\pmod 8$, $(2\omega)^2<q$, $\tau>0$, $2^\alpha>0$, $b-1\leq\gamma$,
$1<b\leq q/2$, $\lfloor b/2\rfloor\leq\gamma$,
$(\mu_0+n_0\lceil\log_b q\rceil)\cdot 2^{\alpha}\leq 2^{m_0}$ and $n_0\leq 2^{m_1}$. Then the
verifier of one iteration, from the bridge through the final sumcheck evaluation, is
coordinate-wise special sound with escape, from the polynomial-evaluation relation to the
reduced evaluation claim on the committed table. Appending the terminal check gives the
complete evaluation argument with the composed certificate.
\end{theorem}

Binding of the scheme and knowledge soundness of its opening, as commitment-scheme properties,
are not formalized. The scheme's terminal verifier and the soundness-side terminal check use
the same decision procedure.
